Human-centred emergency safety

Nirapod

A general-public safety concept with a live survey portal and prototype design work.

HCISurveyResponsive WebFigmaUsability Testing

Problem

People need understandable, thumb-reachable emergency actions in high-stress situations across Dhaka transport and other public contexts.

Constraints

The live deployment is a survey portal. The mobile safety app remains a design prototype unless a working implementation is verified.

Approach

Used contextual and HCI methods to explore an adaptive SOS interface, timed cancellation, offline fallback concepts, and emergency-services integration.

My contribution

I designed and developed the survey experience and contributed to the research and interaction-prototype workflow.

Results

The survey portal is deployed. Aggregate findings can be shown, but respondent-level data is excluded. Survey count and final collection status require verification.

What I would do next

Add verified aggregate usability findings and clear evidence that distinguishes the prototype from a production app.
