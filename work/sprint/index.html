Spatial bioinformatics

SPRINT & Spatial Verification

Spatially grounded communication inference and reproducible feature verification for multiplexed proteomics.

Problem

Spatial molecular measurements need analysis pipelines that preserve tissue structure and distinguish stable features from noise.

Constraints

Methods must be reproducible across graph construction, permutation tests, and split-half verification.

Approach

SPRINT connects imputation, spatial mapping, ligand-receptor inference, and spatial filtering. A separate verification pipeline examines Delaunay graphs, permutation checks, and split-half stability.

My contribution

I worked across the spatial analysis pipeline, reproducibility structure, feature verification, and technical reporting.

Results

Verified result figures are withheld until the exact dataset, cell count, method roles, and interpretation are reconciled.

What I would do next

Document the cohort, graph construction, and stability evidence in a compact audited report.
